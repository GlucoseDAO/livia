## Identity & Positioning

Livia Zaharia is a Romanian architect and parametric jewellery artist who uses script-driven, generative geometry to create nature-evoking wearable objects and installations. She works under the label **Paral Design** (Parametric Livia), translating computational architectural methods into intimate, research-driven, body-scale artefacts.

Her artistic identity sits at the intersection of three domains:

- **Generative art** — code-based, algorithmic form-making rooted in architectural parametric tools
- **Speculative design for health/longevity** — objects framed as "enhancements" at the body/technology/health interface, connected to the Longevity Genie open-source ecosystem
- **Experimental contemporary jewellery** — exhibited at Romanian Jewelry Week (2021–2025), positioned within the international scene of design-driven, conceptual jewellery

She self-describes as architect, parametric designer, jewellery maker, and citizen scientist. She is a contributor to the Longevity Genie open-source ecosystem and founder of GlucoseDAO.

## Practice & Method

Livia uses **parametric and script-driven 3D modelling** with architectural design tools. Her specific stack:

- **Grasshopper** (visual programming environment for Rhino) — primary parametric design tool
- **COMPAS** (https://compas.dev/) — computational framework for architecture/fabrication, with Python scripting
- **Python scripts** — custom generative geometry logic

Her fabrication workflow:

1. **Digital modelling** — base geometries are defined in Grasshopper/COMPAS and transformed into multiple variants via controlled changes in scripts and equations
2. **3D printing** — digital models are materialised via additive manufacturing
3. **Casting** — 3D prints are cast, primarily in **silver** and other metals
4. **Manual assembly** — some pieces incorporate hand-assembled mechanisms, traditional metalworking integration, or mixed-material construction

The output: rings, pendants, earrings, and body-scale objects. Multi-configuration pieces (e.g. objects functioning as both ring and pendant) are common.

## Conceptual Themes

### Nature Mimicry
The central theme. Pieces appear inspired by shells, corals, plants, or cellular structures — yet are generated entirely through code and parametric rules. This raises the question of what counts as "original" versus "copy" when an algorithm produces forms indistinguishable from natural ones.

### Visual Language
- **Fractal** — self-similar structures at multiple scales
- **Biomimetic** — forms that echo biological morphology without directly copying specific organisms
- **Layered surfaces** — complex stacked or nested geometries
- **Repeated modules** — parametric repetition with controlled variation
- **Complex curvature** — non-planar surfaces inviting tactile exploration

### Speculative Enhancements
Through collaborations in the longevity and biotech ecosystem, objects are framed as speculative "enhancements" at the interface of body, technology, and health — blurring the boundaries between jewellery, wearable device, and biotech artefact. This connects her work to the broader field of speculative/critical design.

### Data-to-Form Translation
Some works explicitly translate datasets into physical geometry (e.g. "Cry, Dance, Repeat"), aligning with the Longevity Genie interest in "sequence to function" and data-driven artefacts.

## Ecosystem Connections

### Longevity Genie
Livia is a contributor/citizen scientist within the Longevity Genie open-source ecosystem — an organisation building AI-driven tools for longevity biology. Her "Materialized Enhancements" project bridges physical artefact-making and computational biology infrastructure.

- Main site: https://longevity-genie.github.io
- GitHub: https://github.com/longevity-genie

### GlucoseDAO
Livia is the founder of **GlucoseDAO**, a decentralized autonomous organization focused on open-source glucose prediction benchmarking. The core mission is bridging the gap between machine learning models and human intuition for blood glucose forecasting — no study has systematically compared ML performance against predictions made by people with diabetes drawing on years of lived experience.

**Key components:**
- **Sugar-Sugar Game** (sugar-sugar.glucosedao.org) — a browser-based tool where participants predict 60-minute blood glucose trajectories from real CGM (continuous glucose monitor) data with meal, insulin, and exercise markers. Players receive instant accuracy scores and see how they rank against multiple pre-trained ML models in real time.
- **Open-source data standardization pipeline** — cataloguing and standardizing datasets including HUPA, OhioT1DM, and GlucoBench.
- **Transformer-based models** — benchmarking Informer, NeuralForecaster, Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT), and Gluformer on glucose prediction.
- **Formal human-vs-AI benchmarking study** — ethics clearance received from Universitätsmedizin Rostock (IBIMA).

**Institutional context:** GlucoseDAO operates in collaboration with Universitätsmedizin Rostock (IBIMA). A workshop proposal was submitted to the **47th ISCB & 71st GMDS Joint Annual Conference** (September 27 – October 1, 2026, Freiburg, Germany). Livia's role in the workshop: hands-on session facilitation and audience engagement.

GlucoseDAO demonstrates how decentralized autonomous organizations can support open, reproducible biomedical research — including GDPR-compliant study design, standardization of heterogeneous health data, and rigorous methodology for comparing human and algorithmic performance.

### HEALES (Healthy Life Extension Society)
Livia is affiliated with HEALES. She is listed as a contributor/instructor under this affiliation in conference workshop proposals for ISCB/GMDS 2026.

### ARDD (Aging Research & Drug Discovery)

## Early Pieces & Biographical Details (from ROJW 2021 Interview)

**Origin story:** Livia attended a parametric jewellery design workshop in 2017–2018, where she made her first ring. She is an architect by profession and describes herself as a creator across multiple media — drawing, photography, design, programming, and jewellery. In 2017, with the development of 3D modelling software, she saw the opportunity to conceive and execute jewellery from scratch.

**"Corn" Earrings** — her first significant complex project. Everyone told her it wouldn't work. These were her first cast earrings. The key challenge was the fastening mechanism: she wanted a single-piece design (no soldering), and after researching model-making techniques, she found miniature taps and threading small enough to pass through an ear piercing hole. She redesigned the piece with a straight threaded rod and nut system.

**"Navette" Pendant** — her self-portrait piece. Designed to hold a synthetic alexandrite (a colour-changing stone: reddish-purple in warm light, sapphire blue in cool light) in a marquise cut. "Navette" means "small boat" in French. The pendant is mobile on two axes, shaped as a small boat — representing how everything is subject to change. She considers this her most personally meaningful piece.

**Design philosophy (from interview):** "You shouldn't always choose the known path — you must learn from its lessons and develop from it, drawing inspiration from wherever it may be useful." She emphasizes problem-solving as her core creative drive — friends bring her unusual fabrication challenges, and she has specialized in unconventional forms.

**Material aspiration:** Rose gold — she finds the colour beautiful but hasn't yet designed a piece conceptually suited to it, and notes it requires a finer scale than her usual work.
