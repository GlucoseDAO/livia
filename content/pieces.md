## La Navette Pendant

Cast in silver pendant with free rotation in two directions with synthetic alexandrite.
Its story begins with the stone and I've told it before. What we see in the setting is Alexandrite, a stone that originally came from Russia well known for pleochroism(it changes color depending on the light). My stone swaps between warm purple (warm light) to pale blue of a sapphire (cold light or neon). For more details see the link below.
Its cut, in a flattened ellipse- called navette or marquise-is what inspired the shape. The story says the name marquise comes from a king of France who requested a stone cut to resemble the lips of his mistress- a marquise. And navette comes from how it looks similar to a boat.
That is why I wanted it to move- like a boat on water, with changing colors, or a compass.
