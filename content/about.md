Livia Zaharia is a parametric designer, jewellery maker, and founder of GlucoseDAO. Her work moves between science art, computational form, and digital health, using generative systems to make patterns visible across objects, interfaces, and experiences.

Her design work is presented under [@paral_design](https://www.instagram.com/paral_design/), while the digital health side of her work is expressed through [GlucoseDAO](https://github.com/GlucoseDAO/). Together, these two branches form one interdisciplinary practice.
