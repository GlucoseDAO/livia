# Death Yes No Maybe (Berlin 2026)

*Death? Yes/No/Maybe* is an art and science festival organized by **A Hidden Variable** in Berlin, **20–25 March 2026**, at **Culterim Space Rossmann**.

The programme explores humanity’s pursuit of longevity, immortality, and the technological transformation of the human body. It serves as a platform to ask whether mortality is a necessary condition or a limitation to be overcome, with **50+ international artists and scientists** involved.

**Focus:** The intersection of contemporary art, biomedical science, experimental biohacking, and philosophy around the postponement of death.

**Format:** Visual art, virtual reality (VR/XR), live performances, and panel discussions with artists and researchers.

**Curators:** Ala Leresteux, Lufo Art, and Li Anna Töppe — connecting scientific research with artistic imagination.

**Themes:** Ethical, social, and spiritual dimensions of life extension, human–machine integration, and what it means to die.
