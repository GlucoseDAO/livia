# Spotlight Pavilion

Early large-scale parametric **architecture** project (2019) exploring how digitally designed geometries become physical structures. It prefigures the later miniaturised parametric jewellery work — the bridge from architecture to body-scale artefact.

Her first Romanian Jewelry Week jewellery presentation (2021) is the **Parametric (by) nature** collection — see the **Parametric (by) nature (RJW 2021)** tab.
