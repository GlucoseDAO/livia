# Science-Inspired Jewellery

Ongoing stream drawing from microscopic, cellular, and molecular imagery. Rings, pendants, and earrings echoing cells, tissues, nanoparticles, and organelles.

Includes the Nanot-of-Power pendant (inspired by nanomedicine nanoparticles) and the Mitochondria ring (reimagining mitochondria as sculptural, wearable form).
