# Survival

Centred on the story of being lost in a lush, ever-changing jungle. Deep colours and organic forms reminiscent of potentially dangerous but beautiful plants. Each piece is a tale of survival in a dense forest of shapes, emphasising resilience and adaptation.

Modelled in 3D, cast in silver, mechanisms manually assembled.
