# Parametric (by) nature (RJW 2021)

**Collection concept (Romanian Jewelry Week 2021):** *Parametric (by) nature*

Even though it looks unrestrained, nature has a set of hidden rules. Using those rules, today's computational ability and human inspiration brought these pieces to life. The selection has two parts: **elements** and **entities**. The first is restricted to water for now, in all its forms, based on the characteristics of this element. The second part is mostly related to plants. Each piece was made by 3D modelling and printing and is still unique.

Includes *Ceartari* earrings and rings, *Ice*, *Splash*, and *Fasolaluna (Beanut)* pendants, *Mountain of gold* ring and pendant, and *Mycelium* ring.
