# Flute

Concept proposal exploring how **insulin vials** could be reused and turned into a small **token for people living with diabetes** — a tactile, symbolic object rather than disposable packaging.

The idea was to keep the material memory of daily care while giving it a new narrative: a flute-like form as a quiet, portable emblem.
