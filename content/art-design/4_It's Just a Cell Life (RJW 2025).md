# It's Just a Cell Life (RJW 2025)

**Collection concept (Romanian Jewelry Week 2025):** *It's just a cell life — Biology collection.*

It all started with a cell. It grew, divided and specialized. This collection recalls that: different forms record different stages or parts of the cell. The collection has a start point, the Nucleus, and an end point — Embryo. Not only 3D-printed techniques are used: natural materials are integrated via more traditional methods — walnut husks combined with metal, natural amber trapped in digitally designed settings. Nano particles — a new medical technology — appear as silver, with or without inclusions. Each piece has a story.

The line also includes microscope- and molecule-inspired work: rings, pendants, and earrings echoing cells, tissues, nanoparticles, and organelles — including the Nanot-of-Power pendant (nanomedicine-inspired) and the Mitochondria ring.
