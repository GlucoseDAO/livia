# Spotlight Pavilion

## A Sky for All

**Spotlight Pavilion 2019** — early large-scale parametric **architecture** project exploring how digitally designed geometries become physical structures. It prefigures the later miniaturised parametric jewellery work — the bridge from architecture to body-scale artefact.
