# A world for everyone (RJW 2026)

**Status:** Submitted — in evaluation.

**Collection concept (Romanian Jewelry Week 2026):** *A world for everyone.*

There are multiple worlds occurring at any given time in our presence, all we have to do is open the eyes and find them.

Looking through a bubble like a space in the depths of a lake or freezing through the Eye of Winter, each piece has its own universe. What all of them have in common is that they are glimpses into different coexisting worlds.

3D printed and assembled where the design calls for it; cast silver, glass, PLA, and natural materials — each piece is unique either by combination or by stone.
