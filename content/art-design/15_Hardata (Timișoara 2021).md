# Hardata

**Hardata** was submitted to the **Hyper Form** international **3D-printable sculpture** open call run by **[Lapsus Art Space and Laboratory](https://lapsus607268604.wordpress.com/hyperdome/)** in **Timișoara** (Hyperdome / Hyper Form programme). The work **won first prize**; a later edition invited the author to join the **jury**.

The open-call page describes printing selected works at Lapsus and the prize structure: [Hyper Form — Lapsus](https://lapsus607268604.wordpress.com/hyperdome/).
