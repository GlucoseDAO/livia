# It's Just a Cell Life — Biology Collection

Built around the narrative "it all started with a cell." Includes pieces Nucleus, Embryo, Mitoring, and DarkNut. Forms represent stages of cellular life — division, growth, specialisation, differentiation.

Combines digitally modelled, 3D-printed elements with walnut husks and amber via traditional metalworking.
