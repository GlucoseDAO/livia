# Paths. Memories. Guides (RJW 2022)

**Collection concept (Romanian Jewelry Week 2022):** *Paths. Memories. Guides*

A path that has been set by an event or record. A model to follow which cannot be changed. Memories which record what lies inside us — whether it is a living being, a sound wave or a building. Guides that actively help us find new paths, and the cycle repeats.

Paths fade to memories; memories in turn become guides. All pieces were made using 3D modelling software, 3D printed and cast into silver or laser cut. What makes the difference is the individual backstory of each.

Includes work such as *Sticks and stones* (silver, epidote, prehnite), *La navette* (silver, synthetic alexandrite), *Piguen* (silver, meteorite), *Sunfinder* (plexiglass, Iceland spar), *King's Chapel* (silver, enhanced tourmaline), *Inline*, and *Amberear*.
