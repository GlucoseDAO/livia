# iMAPP — still mapping

**iMapp Bucharest** is a large-scale **projection mapping** festival centred on the **Palace of the Parliament**. Alongside the animated videomapping competition, the festival runs **stillMapp** — a **still-image** (non-animated) mapping strand for single frames mapped onto the façade.

In **2017**, the festival awarded the **stillMapp Award** to **Livia Zaharia** ([official 2017 page](https://imapp.ro/2017-2/): *“Our warmest congratulations go to Livia Zaharia, for submitting one of the best entries in the history of stillMapp…”*).

Participation continued in **still mapping** in **2018**. Edition pages: [iMapp 2017](https://imapp.ro/2017-2/) · [iMapp 2018](https://imapp.ro/2018-2/).
