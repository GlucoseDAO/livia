# The Artifacts of Livia

Every piece forged by Livia carries an alternate-universe fantasy layer — an RPG-flavoured narrative where each artefact is an enchanted item with stats, abilities, and hidden connections. At the heart of it all sits **The Nut of Power**, the supreme artifact that binds and commands every other creation.

### The Dark Nut of Power

<!-- artifact: /RJW2025/IMG_3481.jpg -->

The dark clone of the Nut of Power, forged in shadow. Encased in a blackened walnut husk with copper innards exposed like a wound, this artifact carries a terrible curse — it can curse people that Livia does not like. More dangerously, it sometimes *leaks*, cursing without conscious control.

The leakage is sealed by **Flough** — a seal-type pillow possessed by Anton that functions as a containment device.

**Type:** Pendant | **Materials:** Copper, Walnut | **Size:** 3.4 × 3.4 cm | **Year:** 2024

**Stats:** +5 Dark Arts, +3 Intimidation | **Ability:** Targeted and uncontrolled cursing | **Weakness:** Sealed by Flough

## Character Card — Livia, the Artifactor

**Name:** Livia | **Class:** Artifactor / Herbalist | **Level:** Legendary

Livia is a dual-class wielder: as an **Artifactor**, she forges enchanted artifacts from metal, stone, and organic matter; as a **Herbalist**, she carries a deep love for plants and draws botanical power from the living world.

**Curse:** Type I Diabetes — a persistent debuff that cannot be removed, only managed through alchemy and technology.

**Parentage:** Daughter of the Dark Lord and the Teleporter.

**Superabilities:**

- **Instant Sleep** — Even several minutes of a statistics course triggers immediate, irresistible sleep. This ability is so powerful that no statistics lecture can overcome it... with one exception: *BlueBrown-level statistics*, which is engaging enough to resist the effect.

- **Artifact Forging** — Can create enchanted items from parametric digital models, 3D printing, and traditional metalworking.

- **Herbal Affinity** — Natural bond with plants; can sense their needs and draw strength from green spaces.

## Lore Entities

**Flough** — A seal-type pillow possessed by Anton. Functions as a containment device for the Dark Nut of Power's curse leakage. Without Flough, the Dark Nut's curses would leak uncontrollably into the surrounding world.
