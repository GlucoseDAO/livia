# Treasured Form (Vinnca 2021)

**Year:** 2021

**Venue:** Vinnca — exhibition context celebrating the Vinča cultural legacy, with contemporary jewellery and objects responding to that heritage.

**Status:** Participation — presentation of a piece inspired by the symbolic and material language of Vinča culture.

The submission *Treasured Form* explores volume and ornament in metal — a sculptural reading aligned with that heritage.
