# Shine bright like a star (Osmium 2025)

**Year:** 2025

**Status:** Participation only — the collection did not reach the finalist stage.

**Collection idea (Light-space-shape series)**

Light has no shape. However it reveals the shapes hidden in space. As such any shape will be sculpted by the light.

Since osmium can be considered a pure source of light (at least according to the properties of what is reflected from it), obviously any shape will have to accommodate it.

There are technical limitations, however nothing that human creativity cannot overcome. Any 3D surface can be converted into flat shapes that will protect the light. Shape deconstructing is also a part of the light.

This series is meant to be inspired by space and the cosmic objects that usually are the proving ground of numerous light behaviors — stars. *Shine bright like a diamond* needs to be rewritten. The only measure are the stars.

**A pair of earrings — *Sound of stars*** — where all begins. Stars are formed in the ripples of universe expansion. Sound doesn't travel through space, however light does. In the object created we have both rippling of light and sound. They are just tiny bells.

**A pendant — *White dwarf*** — late stages of star life, when all its essence is dense and heavy. Feel the heaviness of light with an imposing shape meant to gather the attention.

**A ring — *Supernova*** — where all the sparkle that has been gathered during its long life will explode. A core that is protected by the strong outward forces.
