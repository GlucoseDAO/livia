# GlucoseDAO Story

GlucoseDAO is a healthtech startup building tools that help people understand and predict glucose dynamics, with a focus on reducing uncertainty in daily decision-making.

In the Rubik Garage HealthTech interview, Livia Zaharia shares how the idea grew from lived type 1 diabetes experience and continuous glucose monitoring observations during the pandemic. The team then expanded around a shared mission: combine scientific rigor, machine learning, and usable product design to support both people with diabetes and broader metabolic health use cases.

The story also highlights core founder challenges (regulation, strategy, and uncertainty), team formation across science and engineering, and a roadmap that starts with reliable one-hour glucose prediction and may later extend to related biomarkers.
