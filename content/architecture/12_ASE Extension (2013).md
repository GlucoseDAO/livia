# ASE Extension

<!-- artifact: /architecture/covers/ase-extension.jpg -->

- **Type:** university team project, defended March 2013
- **Team:** Adelina Andrei, Mihai Popa, and Livia Zaharia
- **Location:** Piața Romană, Bucharest

The Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE) asked to add two faculties on land it had recently acquired in Piața Romană. With only one month to deliver, the students worked in a team.

The proposal respects the existing buildings and the status of the Nanu Muscel house. It offers a contemporary interpretation of the two earlier volumes.
