# One Mircea Eliade

<!-- artifact: /architecture/covers/one-mircea-eliade.jpg -->

- **Design firm:** XArchitecture
- **Location:** beside Hala Ford, Bucharest. The buildings can be seen from Mircea Eliade Boulevard and Calea Floreasca.
- **Programme:** mixed-use development with three residential towers and one office tower

In 2020 the construction site was nearing completion. Livia worked on the project as part of the office team.

## Livia's role

- Coordinated architecture, structure, and building services while the structure was going up, and again for later changes.
- Designed parts of the ensemble, such as the interior stairs.
- Worked with colleagues on larger parts of the project, including the building terraces and the plaza.
- Handled ceilings, checked façades and exterior elements, and coordinated them with the engineering disciplines, alone or with the team.
