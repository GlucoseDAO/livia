# A Sky for All

<!-- artifact: /architecture/covers/spotlight-2019.jpg -->

- **Type:** competition entry, first prize, April 2019
- **Festival:** [Spotlight 2019](https://spotlightfestival.ro/en/instalatii/a-sky-for-all)

The design won first prize, so it was built and exhibited at the festival. A company associated with the organiser built the installation. Livia stayed in contact with them and supplied the information they needed.

The pavilion has a metal structure, polycarbonate cladding, and adhesive film on the inside. At the end of the festival, it was clear that visitors had enjoyed being inside the space itself.
