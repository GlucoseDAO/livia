# Materialized Enhancements

What would you choose if you could borrow a biological trait from another animal? Regeneration from an axolotl, perhaps, or a tardigrade's resistance to radiation?

[Materialized Enhancements](https://enhancement.bio/) lets you build a character from real genes within a limited credit budget. Each gene comes with research references and a description of how far the evidence has reached. Your choices generate a unique crystal-like 3D form that you can download and print.

The project combines a game, a gene knowledgebase, and bioart. The character is imagined; the scientific references help you explore the biology behind it.
