# Art you can explore

These projects invite you to take part. Choose genes and make a printable form, walk through a town built from jewellery, or hear an orchestra made from whale and dolphin recordings.

## Materialized Enhancements

Pick biological traits from real animals and see your choices become a unique 3D form. A game about possible human futures, with research behind each gene and an object you can print.

[Explore Materialized Enhancements](/digital-art/materialized-enhancements) · [Try it at enhancement.bio](https://enhancement.bio/)

## Livistone

Livia's rings and pendants grow into buildings in a town you can walk through. Find the original pieces, read their stories, and visit places inspired by her art and science projects.

[Discover Livistone](/digital-art/livistone) · [Enter the town](https://livistone.liviazaharia.com/)

## Whale and Dolphin Orchestra

Choose real recordings on an ocean map and hear music shaped by the animals' rhythms and pitch. This working prototype also explores how a recorded call could shape a piece of jewellery.

[Meet the orchestra](/digital-art/whale-and-dolphin-orchestra) · [Listen and explore](https://whales.liviazaharia.com/)
