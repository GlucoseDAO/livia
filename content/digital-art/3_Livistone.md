# Livistone

Walk into a town where rings and pendants have become buildings.

In [Livistone](https://livistone.liviazaharia.com/), Livia's jewellery grows to the scale of architecture. Arrive at an amber station, pass through a ring-shaped gate, and explore a town whose buildings begin with objects she has made. A walnut shell becomes City Hall; silver folds around amber become the Ministry of Energy.

<!-- artifact: /digital-art/livistone.jpg -->

You can walk through the town or use the map to jump between places. Displays show photographs of the original pieces and their stories. Her research projects have places of their own, too. The town and the powers it gives its objects are fiction, rooted in real artworks and research.

The background music is Livia playing kalimba, recorded on her phone.
