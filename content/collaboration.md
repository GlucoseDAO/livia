## Invite me as an artist

I'd be happy to hear about an exhibition, a residency, or an artwork we could make together. I work with parametric jewellery, objects, and digital art, often using ideas from biology. A collaboration can start with a material, a place, or a question you're curious about.

You can see examples in [Collections](/art-design) and [Digital Art](/digital-art).

## Freelance projects

Have a piece in mind, need a 3D model, or want to turn an idea into an interactive experience? You can approach me about a commission or freelance project. My work includes computational design, jewellery, and turning digital forms into physical objects.

Tell me what you want to make. If you have references, a deadline, or a budget in mind, those help too. It's fine if you're still working those out.

## Buy or design a piece

Some of my jewellery is for sale. If a piece catches your eye in the [Pieces catalogue](/pieces), just ask. Send me its name or a photo, and I can tell you about availability and price.

You can also get in touch to design a piece with me. Tell me what you have in mind, even if it's just a starting idea, and we can work on it together.

## Research together

I'm also open to scientific collaboration on **glucose prediction, agentic AI, and genomics**. That might mean comparing glucose forecasts, building AI agents that help with research tasks, or exploring gene-expression data. I also enjoy practical workshops where we learn by making something.

I founded [GlucoseDAO](/science-tech/glucosedao) and contribute to the [Longevity Genie](/science-tech/longevity-genie) ecosystem. You can read more about that work in [Science & Tech](/science-tech).
